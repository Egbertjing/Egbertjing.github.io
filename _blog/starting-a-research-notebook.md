Research pages are good at showing finished work. They are less good at keeping
track of the questions, failed attempts, and small observations that lead to it.
I am opening this notebook for those in-between ideas.

## What I plan to write about

The first notes will likely stay close to my research: language agents, safety
and privacy, evaluation, and the systems needed to make agents useful without
losing control of their context.

I also want room for reading notes, small experiments, and practical lessons
that do not need to become papers before they are worth sharing.

## A deliberately unfinished space

Entries here may be shorter and more provisional than publications. I will
revise them as my understanding changes and use this page as an index of that
process.
