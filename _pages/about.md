I am **Huihao Jing**, a Computer Science PhD student at the
[Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/)
(HKUST). I work with [Haoran Li](https://hlibt.student.ust.hk/) and am advised
by Prof. [Yangqiu Song](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~yqsong/) in the
[KnowComp Group](https://github.com/HKUST-KnowComp).

My research focuses on **LLM agents** and **AI safety**. I am especially
interested in how agents reason, use tools, manage context, and preserve safety
properties throughout long and complex workflows.

Research interests
------

* LLM agents and agent infrastructure
* AI safety, privacy, and contextual integrity
* Multi-agent and long-horizon reasoning
* Policy, legal, and compliance reasoning
