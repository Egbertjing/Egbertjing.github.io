My work studies language agents as complete systems rather than isolated model
outputs. I care about what an agent can access, what it remembers, how it uses
tools, and how it recovers when something goes wrong.

Safe agent systems
------

Modern agents connect language models with tools, files, credentials, memory,
and external services. My research explores the system boundaries needed to
make these agents safer, including isolation, permissions, contextual
integrity, state management, tracing, and recovery.

Agent reasoning and evaluation
------

Final task accuracy often hides where an agent succeeds or fails. I design
benchmarks and analyses that examine the full trajectory: how agents search,
coordinate, apply rules, and turn intermediate reasoning into final actions.

Contextual safety and compliance
------

Safety and privacy decisions depend on context. I study how models can reason
over policies, laws, social roles, and information flows instead of relying
only on fixed lists of harmful content.
