<div class="intro-panel">
  <p class="page-kicker">Research agenda</p>
  <p class="intro-lead">I study language agents as complete systems rather than isolated model outputs.</p>
  <p>This means looking at what an agent can access, what it remembers, how it uses tools, and how it recovers when something goes wrong.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-themes">
  <article class="research-theme">
    <span class="theme-index">01</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Safe agent systems</h2>
      <p>Modern agents connect language models with tools, files, credentials, memory, and external services. I study system boundaries for safer agents, including isolation, permissions, contextual integrity, state management, tracing, and recovery.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
  <article class="research-theme">
    <span class="theme-index">02</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Agent reasoning and evaluation</h2>
      <p>Final task accuracy often hides where an agent succeeds or fails. I design benchmarks and analyses that examine full trajectories: how agents search, coordinate, apply rules, and turn intermediate reasoning into final actions.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
  <article class="research-theme">
    <span class="theme-index">03</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Contextual safety and compliance</h2>
      <p>Safety and privacy decisions depend on context. I study how models can reason over policies, laws, social roles, and information flows instead of relying only on fixed lists of harmful content.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
</div>
