<div class="intro-panel">
  <p class="page-kicker">Research agenda</p>
  <p class="intro-lead">My research focuses on LLM agents and LLM safety.</p>
  <p>I study how agents become more capable in real systems, and how they can remain safe when working with tools, context, other agents, and external environments.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-themes">
  <article class="research-theme">
    <span class="theme-index">01</span>
    <div>
      <h2>LLM Agents</h2>
      <p>I study the systems and reasoning methods that allow language agents to use tools, discover capabilities, coordinate with other agents, and complete complex tasks. My recent work covers agent harnesses, active tool discovery, multi-agent reasoning, and agent evaluation.</p>
      <p class="research-work"><span>Representative work</span> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16591">SING</a>, <a href="https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.31393.57447">A Survey on AI Agent Harness</a>, and <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24922">MASLegalBench</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
  <article class="research-theme">
    <span class="theme-index">02</span>
    <div>
      <h2>LLM Safety</h2>
      <p>I study safety beyond input and output filtering. My work examines system boundaries, contextual integrity, privacy, policy reasoning, and legal compliance for both language models and agents.</p>
      <p class="research-work"><span>Representative work</span> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12406">Isolation for LLM-Agent Safety</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14590">MCIP</a>, <a href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.294/">ContextLens</a>, <a href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.115/">OmniCompliance-100K</a>, and <a href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.516/">PrivaCI-Bench</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
</div>

<div class="research-approach">
  <p class="page-kicker">Research approach</p>
  <p>Across both directions, I build benchmarks, datasets, system protocols, and empirical analyses. These tools help reveal where models and agents fail, and whether a proposed method improves the behavior that matters in practice.</p>
</div>
